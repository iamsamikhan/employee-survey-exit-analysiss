# 📊 Employee Exit Survey Analysis

## 🚀 Overview

This project analyzes employee exit survey data to uncover patterns of dissatisfaction across different employee groups. The goal is to identify key factors influencing employee attrition and provide data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Organizations often struggle to understand **why employees leave**.
This project answers:

* Which employees are most dissatisfied?
* Does experience level affect dissatisfaction?
* What patterns exist across service categories?

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

* Python 🐍
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib

---

## 🔧 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Key steps performed:

* Merged multiple datasets (TAFE & DETE surveys)
* Handled missing values and inconsistencies
* Standardized column names
* Converted categorical responses into structured format
* Engineered a new feature: **`dissatisfied`**

---

## 📊 Key Analysis

### 1. Feature Engineering

* Extracted numeric tenure from text (e.g., "3-4 years")
* Converted boolean indicators into meaningful insights

---

### 2. Grouping & Aggregation

Used pivot tables to calculate dissatisfaction rates:

👉 Calculated the **percentage of dissatisfied employees per category**

---

## 📈 Visualization

* Bar chart comparing dissatisfaction across service categories

**Insight:**

* Established & Veteran employees show higher dissatisfaction
* New employees show the lowest dissatisfaction

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* 📌 Employee dissatisfaction increases with experience
* 📌 Long-term employees are more likely to leave due to dissatisfaction
* 📌 Early-stage employees are relatively satisfied

---

## 💡 Business Impact

These insights can help organizations:

* Improve employee retention strategies
* Focus on experienced employees at risk of leaving
* Design better workplace policies
